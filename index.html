Startup data, with sources.

Import startup information only from sources your fund may use, keeping source links, capture dates and usage rights.

Sources →Startup records

Resolving what's missing.

Duplicate company records are merged. Missing or conflicting facts are flagged, never guessed.

Startup records →Data check

Closing gaps with evidence.

Gaps are filled from permitted sources and keep their links. Until then, unknown facts stay unknown and are not scored as negative.

Flagged →Sourced

Eligible, then scored.

Hard eligibility rules run first. Then an explainable fit score is calculated from the evidence available.

Evidence →Fit score

A shortlist for your model.

Eligible startups are ranked against your versioned model: stage, geography, sectors, check size, exclusions and weighted criteria.

Scored →Shortlist

Returns you can inspect.

Adjust the assumptions and see the calculation, not only the answer. Gross, deal-level scenarios from dated cash flows and simple dilution.

Startup →Return scenario

Review, annotate, share.

The best match stays linked to its return assumptions. Share the decision record with named recipients. Fit shows as criteria matched, and people own the decision.

Review →Decision record
